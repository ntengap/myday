MA VIE EN FRANÇAIS - INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE

1. Upload ALL files in this folder to any website folder that uses https,
   for example  https://yoursite.com/mavie/
   (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or your own hosting all work.)
2. Open  https://yoursite.com/mavie/  on your phone.
   - iPhone (Safari): Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
   - Android (Chrome): menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3. Open it once with internet. After that it works offline.
   Your uses and progress stay on that phone.

UPDATING: after you upload a new index.html, change VERSION in sw.js
(for example "mavie-v2") so phones pick up the new version.

Note: opening index.html straight from the Files app will not install it.
It must be served from a website over https.
